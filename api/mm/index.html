    Multi-modal¶

    Multi-modal single cell: ADT counts, their PCA and the WNN graph.

    Adds a protein modality to a SingleCellsMultiModal, normalises it (CLR or DSB), runs a PCA over it and fuses it with the RNA through a weighted nearest neighbour (WNN) graph. The RNA side is a plain SingleCells, so every RNA function takes the object as it is.

    Start with add_adt_counts, then calculate_pca_adt. Run the RNA PCA the usual way (bv.pp.find_hvg, bv.pp.calculate_pca) and call generate_wnn_graph. From there bv.graph.find_clusters and bv.embed take modality="wnn"; modality="adt" does the same for the protein side alone.

    What lands where. add_adt_counts cuts the counts to the kept cells, normalises them and stores them in mm.adt; it also writes the var_adt table to the DuckDB and adt_nnz and adt_lib_size to obs. The counts stay in memory, not in the count store, and bv.io.save keeps them. calculate_pca_adt writes scores, loadings and singular values to mm.adt_cache. generate_wnn_graph puts the fused kNN, its graph and the per-cell modality weights in mm.wnn. new_adt_counts_clr, new_adt_counts_dsb and the get_* functions return their result and leave the experiment alone.

    # adt_counts: dense cells x proteins array; barcodes and proteins: their labels
mm = bv.SingleCellsMultiModal(sc.dir)
bv.mm.add_adt_counts(mm, adt_counts, barcodes, proteins, method="clr")
bv.pp.find_hvg(mm, hvg_no=500)
bv.pp.calculate_pca(mm, no_pcs=20)
bv.mm.calculate_pca_adt(mm, no_pcs=10)
bv.mm.generate_wnn_graph(mm)
bv.graph.find_clusters(mm, modality="wnn")
weights = bv.mm.get_wnn_weights(mm)

    Gotchas. Every kept cell needs a row in the ADT barcodes, or add_adt_counts raises. The matrix is cut to the kept cells when you add it: dropping cells afterwards is fine, putting dropped cells back is not (calculate_pca_adt then raises). DSB wants the isotype controls by default (DsbParams.use_isotype_controls), so pass isotype_names, or switch the option off. Leave the isotypes out of the ADT PCA with features=. generate_wnn_graph needs the PCA of both modalities, rebuilds the per-modality kNN graphs itself at knn_range neighbours rather than reusing the cached ones, and replaces any earlier WNN result with its embeddings.

    Worked through in Multi-modal single cell analysis with bixverse.

    Weighted nearest neighbour graph over two modalities.

    Per-cell modality weights come from comparing within- and cross-modality neighbourhood distances; they fuse the two into one kNN. The per-modality kNN graphs are always rebuilt from the embeddings at knn_range neighbours, the cached ones are not reused. The fused kNN (pseudo-distances), its neighbour graph and the weights replace mm.wnn, and any WNN embeddings with them; use modality="wnn" to cluster or lay it out.
